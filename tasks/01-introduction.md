## Notes
Nothing to configure here, but it's worth pointing out why the lab setup matters in the first place: everything in this room (and in any hands-on security work) runs against an isolated VM, cut off from any production network. That isolation is what makes it safe to run recon commands, mess with permissions, or test payloads without any risk of touching a real system. It's the same principle behind sandboxes and disposable VMs used in professional pentesting — never test on infrastructure you can't afford to break.
